Nuclear Glow Up!

Unstable nuclei glow up into stable ones. Fire decays, block radiation, watch half-lives tick, and start a chain reaction.

📘 How to use this game
  • Start with ⚛️ Decay Lab: tap α, β⁻, β⁺, EC or γ and watch the nucleus and its equation change.
  • Gold boxes say 👉 what to do in the lab. Do it, watch, then answer.
  • ⚖️ Balance It! has boxes: type the top number (mass), the bottom number (atomic number) and the symbol.
  • 💡 Hint gives a clue. 👀 Show Answer reveals it but earns no point. 🎯 Skill Check gives you a study plan.
